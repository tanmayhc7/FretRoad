# Fretroad

A free, open-source, story-driven game that teaches the guitar fretboard to intermediate players.
You ride a night road with an old session player, Marlow. Every stop is a stretch of the neck:
a short lesson, a puzzle on a live fretboard, a quiz, and (later) a practical test played on your own guitar.

Everything runs in the browser. Nothing is uploaded. No accounts.

## Try the prototype

Open `prototype/index.html` in a browser (double-click it). Turn your sound on.

Current build: Chapter 1, three stops (find the A, the fifth above, a three-string major chord),
lessons before each puzzle, and a sandbox for scales.
